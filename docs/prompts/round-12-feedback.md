# Round 12 feedback (Tanvir, 2026-11-25)

A summary of Tanvir's twelfth review, written in Bangla and English, as a reusable brief. Nineteen items.

## Products

1. **Letter.** Make the photo bigger, about half the letter. Use the old ghost photo (the witch), not the dark corridor where nothing can be made out. "REMEMBER ME" is cut off on the right: move it, or put it under the bigger photo (there is room). Play the whole thing a little faster. Make the letter paper a little taller.
2. **Doll.** The head should come upright a bit faster, but still slowly. The blood from the eyes should run slowly. Give each eye a black pupil; the red ring can stay around it.
3. **Home page.** Find a random blood-spot-like mark and remove it.
4. **Mirror.** Opening its full view makes the mirror jerk as it appears. Fix it.
5. **Typewriter.** The paper is too narrow: make it wider (and taller if needed). The typed text cannot be read: make it bigger and darker. Make "Please, Help!!" bigger.
6. **Telephone.** The blood falls from the back of the handset. It must come out of the earpiece holes and drip the same way it does now.
7. **Calendar.** The cross looks like a child drew it. Make it two quick straight pen strokes, in the same colour as the Saint name. Each stroke is thinner where it starts and ends, and the last stroke is much longer. Keep the name's style but make it blood red. Make the cut through the middle longer on both sides and a little thicker. As soon as the cut appears, the letters start to melt and run down a little (not a long blood trail).
8. **Rocking chair.** It still looks like an ordinary chair. Make it reclined and comfortable, like a chair you lie back in. The padding looks raised, like foam: use thin classic upholstery cloth that dents the way cloth does when someone sits or lies on it, in a colour where the dents show.
9. **Camera.** Whatever way it has been turned, it must come round to face the screen straight on before it takes the photo, and a lens cover must slide aside to take it. Make "you, tonight" on the print clearer. The camera's ghost, the envelope's photo, the frame's ghost, the mirror and the globe's hands are related; the story will be tied together later (do not write it now). Bring back the old letter ghost, improved.
10. **Music box.** The spring in the middle, whose skull flew out, stays in the box and keeps springing like the others. The skull should not slow down on screen; it just goes.
11. **Candle.** The smoke ghost should have no hands and no defined head or shoulders: a hollow face in the smoke, eye sockets, nose and an open mouth as holes in the smoke. No dramatic entrance or exit: it forms in the smoke and rises away naturally. Make the darkness around the candle a little stronger, about what one candle in the dark would show.
12. **Teddy.** The slash appears first, and the neck parts almost at the same moment, as soon as the slash is there.

## Whole site

13. **Transition.** At the end of the Christmas-to-Halloween transition the bat seems stuck in front of you before Halloween arrives. It should just keep going, the dark comes, then Halloween. The music-box skull does the same (it stops at your face as if to say "I'm here"): it should go straight past.
14. **3D lag (the biggest task).** Every 3D item, the hero included, lags when the page scrolls: it seems to float off the page and then catch up. It must stay fixed to the page. Also, when the site's lights dim, the 3D items glow on their own as if floating over the dark page.
15. **Remove** the fallen tin soldier, the glass ornament and the cake.
16. **New products**, each with a Christmas version, a Halloween version and a Take event: an hourglass; Santa's reindeer and sleigh; Aladdin's lamp, from which a ghost comes out instead of a genie; and three or four more classic, ghost-related products that fit the story.
17. **Hero.** A frightening scarecrow with a pumpkin head, creaking in the wind, its bones and clothes moving.
18. **Distance.** A werewolf far away that now and then lifts its head and howls.
19. **Footer graveyard.** On one side a skeleton trying to pull another skeleton out of a grave; on the other side a skeleton crawling.
