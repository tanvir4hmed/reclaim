# Round 8: per-product Take events (summary of Tanvir's request)

1. **Pocket watch.** The full view and the shop card always show the same time. Before the take it keeps the visitor's own time, in the Christmas shop, during the transformation and in the haunted shop. After the take it jumps to ten to three at night (2:50) and runs backward from there.
2. **Doll.** Blood seeps slowly from her eyes, the way the typewriter's words seep in. Before the take her eyes look away to a corner; after it they look straight out at the visitor in the middle of the screen.
3. **Letter.** The flap tears open from the wax seal and stays open (it used to vanish). The page rises only to just below the "YOU KILLED ME" stain; the rest stays in the envelope.
4. **Cake.** More realistic. After the take a slice is missing, with clean cut faces. The fly leaves smoothly and is recognisable as a fly.
5. **Shoes.** Footprints appear only inside the shoe's full view. On returning to the shop they stop, and only the prints made during the full view stay. The haunted leather looks dusty, aged and worn instead of glossy gold.
6. **Typewriter.** "Please, Help!!" replaces "Kill Me", seeping in slowly like a blood stain.
7. **Telephone.** Blood runs down the phone's surface realistically. The dial jiggles left and right a little, at random.
8. **Advent calendar.** After Take It the view stays open. The cross is drawn slowly in blood, with no drips. "Saint Nicholas" is written in the space below, and then claw marks tear through the middle of the name: black underneath, rough edges.
9. **Camera.** An instant-print camera. After the take it turns slowly from wherever it is to face the middle of the screen, flashes once, and slowly (not too slowly) prints the existing photograph.
10. **Surprise box.** A broken, aged, frightening skull springs out and keeps bouncing. The coloured pieces fall onto the surface and stay.
11. **Rocking chair.** Keep the rocking. Add the pressure of someone invisible on the seat, back and arms while it keeps rocking.
12. **Snow globe.** The ghost is a dark shadow with hands, its lower body thinning away like a genie from a lamp. It presses its hands on the glass, wants out and beats its head against it.
13. **Chocolates.** A slow, smooth change from truffles to skulls; more realistic skulls and textures.
14. **Music box.** The lid is open in the full view but closed in the shop; make them match. After the take several skulls spring out, and one comes out toward the middle of the screen, growing like the bat's lunge but without covering the whole screen, and passes through.
15. **Glass ornament.** It must read as an ornament, and the ghost stays inside it, not outside.
16. **Teddy bear.** The cut is at the throat and the head comes off realistically. Blood across the bear and around it, disturbing and realistic, as if it were a living person.
