# Round 10: the catch flight (summary of Tanvir's request)

1. After the click, the bat turns upright and then comes straight at the screen; that looks robotic. Instead it should start flying the way it flies on the Christmas page, and come at the screen with its wings spread, for the transition.
2. The wings should spread wider in flight; now they look roundish, which bat wings are not. They should reach further out to both sides.
3. The wings should be much bigger than the body, as a real bat's are.
4. It should come a little faster, and a little higher (by about 10 % of the screen).
5. When it is right in front, its wings should cover the top of the screen too; at the moment the transition happens while the top is still empty.
