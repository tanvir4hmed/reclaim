# Round 6: bat refinement (summary of Tanvir's brief)

Refine the existing bat (do not redesign it):

1. Scale the whole bat to 80 % of the deployed Christmas bat, keeping the aspect ratio and the hanging anchor.
2. Ears: 50 % of the original absolute length. Inside the already scaled drawing this is 0.5 / 0.8 = 0.625; do not compound reductions. Attach them to the sides of the head, angled outward, with the crown visible between and beyond the roots.
3. A distinguishable head, short neck and shoulders. Pointed, anatomically believable wing corners where the folded wings change direction, with shade under them and a lit ridge.
4. Layered wing colour: a dark reddish-brown base, deep fold shadows, warmer raised ridges, low-contrast bones and veins, subtle texture, and controlled edges with no plastic outlines. Suggested palette: #110704 to #9B675B.
5. Better visibility of the eyes, nose and mouth through contrast, without changing the expression. Fur palette: #1D0D08 to #5F3829.
6. Slender legs and hooked claws, not human-like hands. Palette: #210A05 to #99644A.
7. Christmas and Halloween bats use one shared resting size and anchor, so there is no jump or resize between modes.
8. Verify by measurement and close-ups at the real display size, on desktop and smaller screens.
9. Most important: keep the original frightening expression.

Also: the pocket watch must run backward only after it is taken, both on the card and in the full product view.
