# Re:Claim: round 4 feedback (prompt)

Implement every item below. Do not drop any.

## The bat

1. **Sizes.** By day the hanging bat is slightly smaller than the haunted shop's hanging bats. When it flies it starts at its hanging size, as if it were far away, grows as it swoops across the front of the screen, and shrinks again as it lands, as if it were flying off and settling somewhere far away. Its largest size in flight equals the current hanging size.
2. **Hanging shape (follow the reference photo).** Two pointed shoulder corners, at about the same height as the head, with the head hanging between them. The folded wings must read as one arm wrapped over the other: the wing on top runs diagonally across the body. Show a gap between the two legs at the top.
3. **Gaze.** Bring back the earlier behaviour: now and then it looks toward wherever the mouse is. When the mouse is over it, keep the current reaction (eyes and mouth open).
4. **The catch.** After the catch it flies right across the full screen and leaves two claw scratch marks, then the transformation happens. As its wings come closer it gets darker. It should not look red: a little red can show, but it is mostly dark.
