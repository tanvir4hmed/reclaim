# Round 7: bat flight, hand size, lunge, flying bat (summary of Tanvir's request)

1. In flight the bat must not grow and then shrink and snap back to its hanging size. It flies at the scale it hangs at: wings spread, so the body looks a little smaller and the wings bigger, and it lands at the same scale.
2. The skeleton-hand cursor at half its current size (about twice a normal cursor).
3. The catch lunge starts from the bat's own size and flies at the screen until it goes dark, as now.
4. The flying bat follows a detailed brief ("Final Horror Bat"): browser-rendered (SVG), very wide wings rising to two hooked tips, torn lower edges and holes, layered dark burgundy membrane (recesses between bones, raised folds, veins, joint highlights), near-black fur, pointed ears, small amber-orange eyes without a halo, the snarl with fangs, two legs hanging relaxed with small claws (not reaching forward). During the approach the face is the anchor, centred at about 42–45 % of the viewport height; the darkness must come from the creature itself, not an unrelated black flash.

The brief refers to an approved reference image that was not attached; the drawing follows its written description.
